# Privacy Policy — Mindset (iOS app)

Last updated: August 2026 · This version is intended for publication as a public URL
(App Store mandatory field). It is the English counterpart of the German version and says
the same thing: [Deutsche Fassung / German version](https://applabs.bruckschen.eu/mindset/de/privacy).

## Summary

Mindset does not collect, gather or transmit any personal data to the provider or to third
parties. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no trackers and no provider
server.

## Data storage

All content you create (principles, versions, reflections, mood notes, daily review entries,
categories) is created by you and stored locally on your device (iOS Data Protection).

## iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable private iCloud sync, Apple stores your content encrypted in your own private
iCloud (CloudKit Private Database). Only your Apple ID has access; the provider cannot read
your content. Apple's privacy terms for iCloud apply in addition. You can switch the sync off
in the app at any time. For the strongest protection you can additionally enable Advanced Data
Protection for iCloud in your iOS settings (end-to-end encryption).

## Apple Intelligence (optional)

AI features run entirely on your device (Apple Foundation Models). No content is transmitted
to the provider or to third parties for this purpose.

## Siri and Shortcuts (optional)

When you use Siri capture, Apple processes your voice input in accordance with Apple's privacy
terms; the recognised text is stored exclusively locally within the app.

## Notifications (optional)

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. For an impulse you decide in the app whether
the notification shows any content. The daily review reminder never shows content from your
entries.

## TestFlight (beta versions only)

If you use a beta version through TestFlight, feedback you send (screenshots, comments, device
data) is processed by Apple under the TestFlight terms and made available to the developer for
debugging. It is deleted from development systems within 30 days at the latest. This does not
apply to the version published in the App Store.

## Your rights (GDPR)

You have the following rights. Because the provider never receives your data, you exercise most
of them directly in the app rather than by making a request to us.

Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): the provider holds no personal data about you that could be
disclosed. You can see all of your data in the app at any time.
Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): every entry can be edited directly in the app.
Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): delete entries individually, or delete the app to remove local
data; iCloud data is deleted via your iCloud settings or by switching the sync off.
Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): you can switch iCloud sync off at any time,
after which processing takes place exclusively on your device.
Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): a complete export in a machine-readable format is
available in the app at any time (Profile → Export).
Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): there is no processing based on legitimate interests that could be
objected to — no advertising, no profiling, no analytics. Optional features (iCloud sync,
notifications, Siri) can be switched off in the app at any time.
Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR): you may lodge a complaint with a data protection
supervisory authority — either the one responsible for your place of residence or the authority
responsible for the provider, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of
Information of North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany.

Because no data is transmitted to the provider, requests for access, rectification or erasure
addressed to the provider have no data to act upon — your data is held by you alone.
